❄ Mark a square when you spot it · Five in a row wins ❄

Tap a square when you spot it. This page's URL holds your card, so bookmark it to come back to the same one.

Drinking rules

Optional and always 21+. Water, cocoa, or cider counts the same as anything stronger. Pace yourself, don't drive, and nobody has to drink to play.

The basics

  • Mark a square: 1 sip.
  • Complete a line (row, column, or diagonal): give out 3 sips. Say "Bingo!" loudly.
  • Blackout (every square): finish your drink and pick the next movie.
  • The one kiss: when the "No kissing until the end" square pays off, everyone drinks.
